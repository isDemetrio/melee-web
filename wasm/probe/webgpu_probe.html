WebGPU probe

Three questions, all answered into window.__probe for the CI check to read: does this browser expose a usable WebGPU adapter, device and readback (adapter, device, round_trip, readback), can the pinned Emscripten build and run a WebGPU translation unit (cxx), and can C++ adopt the device this page acquired in JavaScript before the module ran (adopt)? The same adoption is asked again of the worker's own realm, where the renderer instantiates its module (adopt_worker). The canvas is configured, cleared and submitted, and what its commit did to the device is recorded in canvas_commit rather than required — see the worker's header.
